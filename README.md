# SME Customer Segmentation Using K-Means Clustering

## Project Overview

This project applies K-Means Clustering, a machine learning technique, to segment customers into distinct groups based on their Annual Income and Spending Score. The goal is to help Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) better understand customer behavior and make data-driven business decisions.

Customer segmentation enables businesses to identify their most valuable customers, improve marketing strategies, increase customer retention, and maximize revenue.

---

## Problem Statement

Many SMEs struggle to understand the different types of customers they serve. As a result, they often use the same marketing approach for all customers, leading to inefficient spending and missed business opportunities.

This project addresses that challenge by grouping customers with similar purchasing behaviors into meaningful segments.

---

## Project Objectives

The objectives of this project are to:

## Methodology

### 1. Data Loading
The dataset was loaded into a Pandas DataFrame for analysis.

### 2. Data Exploration
The dataset structure, dimensions, and missing values were examined.

### 3. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
Visualizations were created to understand customer income and spending behavior.

### 4. Elbow Method
The Elbow Method was used to determine the optimal number of customer clusters.

### 5. K-Means Clustering
Customers were grouped into five distinct segments based on income and spending patterns.

### 6. Customer Profiling
Each cluster was analyzed and assigned a business-friendly segment name.

## Customer Segments Identified

### VIP Customers
- High Income
- High Spending
- Most valuable customer group

### High Income Low Spending Customers
- High purchasing power
- Opportunity for targeted promotions

### Low Income High Spending Customers
- Highly engaged customers
- Responsive to discounts and special offers

### Low Income Low Spending Customers
- Budget-conscious customers
- Suitable for affordable product offerings

### Average Customers
- Moderate income and spending behavior
- Largest customer segment

---

## Key Findings

- Five distinct customer groups were identified.
- VIP Customers generated the highest business value.
- High Income Low Spending Customers represented an untapped revenue opportunity.
- Low Income High Spending Customers showed strong purchasing behavior despite lower income levels.
- Customer segmentation can improve marketing effectiveness and customer retention.

## Recommendations
